The mandate

Speed is essential.
Control is non-negotiable.

ClearGlassInc Artemis is a self-evolving intelligence platform built for secure, coalition-aware, multi-domain operations. It fuses live and historical evidence, reasons across governed knowledge, and continuously proposes better ways to work.

The system never changes its own mission or authority. It may propose prompt, workflow, heuristic, and routing improvements—but promotion remains content-bound, evaluated, approved, reversible, and fully auditable.

“Machine speed should compress the distance between evidence and understanding—not the distance between recommendation and accountability.”

AI & agent design

Bounded autonomy.
Compounding intelligence.

Agents exchange provenance-bearing artifacts—not hidden conversational memory. Tools use typed contracts, short-lived delegated credentials, classification ceilings, and strict time and cost limits.
